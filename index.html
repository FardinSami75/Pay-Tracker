Pay rates & break rules

›
  • University AV/IT: no break at 5 hours or less, 30 min over 5 hours, 60 min at 8 hours or more.
  • Winners: no break at 5 hours or less, 30 min up to 6.5 hours, 45 min over 6.5 hours.
  • Stampede: no break at 5 hours or less, 30 min over 5 hours.
  • Worked hours past 8 in a shift pay 1.5×.
  • Winners only: every hour on an Alberta general holiday pays 1.5× (New Year's Day, Family Day, Good Friday, Victoria Day, Canada Day, Labour Day, Thanksgiving, Remembrance Day, Christmas Day).
